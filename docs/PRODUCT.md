# CivicOps AI

## Register

product

## Users

Citizens reporting local civic issues from mobile or desktop, in English, Urdu or
Roman Urdu. Operators review persistent incidents, evidence, departments, response
plans and status changes. The repository documents verified FastAPI/Supabase flows.

## Product Purpose

Make civic reporting simple and operational review clear. Text reports may be
anonymous; private evidence and owned-report features require a citizen account.
AI classification and incident intelligence assist human operational decisions.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, composed. A professional civic operations platform.

## Anti-references

Generic hackathon admin templates, bright red branding, oversized map-led dashboards,
narrow mobile cards surrounded by empty desktop space, fabricated metrics or evidence.

## Design Principles

- Describe, locate, optionally attach evidence, then submit.
- Keep the operational queue and incident intelligence central.
- Distinguish AI recommendations from human decisions at the point of action.
- Preserve real data, truthful states, security and the existing operational contract.
- Use progressive detail and compact hierarchy without hiding essential context.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, labeled controls, responsive layouts,
reduced-motion support and visible English/Urdu/Roman Urdu selection. Status must
remain understandable without color. User-approved brief guides this redesign.
